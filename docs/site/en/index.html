One platform, three everyday roles

Members

Sign in, manage devices and review eligibility and usage, then manually select an authorized exit in the official app.

Administrators

Manage members and exit nodes. Follow publication, access withdrawal and the observed results of asynchronous operations.

Operators

Deploy central services and nodes, apply signed updates, and follow runbooks for backup and recovery.

Existing implementation scope

From joining the service
to maintaining every exit.

NetBird supplies the overlay and existing clients. Freedom VPN adds service management and enforcement through upstream APIs and the CLI. Acceptance records define how far each capability is validated.

The member connection path

Use the official NetBird apps.

The member portal handles accounts and devices. Official apps handle VPN connections and exit selection. This project does not ship its own branded client.

  1. Check service entitlement

    Sign in to the member portal and review the plan, device allowance and available exits.

  2. Connect to self-hosted management

    Follow the member guide to set the NetBird management URL and sign in with the same OIDC identity.

  3. Select an authorized exit

    After device authorization, manually choose an exit in the official app. Review usage and fair-use status in the member portal.

Readiness & verification

Implemented does not mean
production-validated.

Freedom VPN is in development and validation. The delivery guide and acceptance records are authoritative; this page does not announce a production release or universal platform support.

Devices & networks

Windows, macOS, Android, iOS, physical arm64, candidate hardware capacity and some isolation and failure scenarios still have validation gaps.

Member-device protection

Server-side Internet-only isolation does not provide an OS kill switch. A disconnected tunnel does not guarantee the device stops using its original ISP.

Production configuration

Production IdP / MFA, payment providers, domains / TLS, DNS lists and operating parameters still need separate configuration and validation.
