The member connection path

Official apps. Your own service.

The Freedom VPN member portal handles subscriptions and devices. Official NetBird apps handle VPN connections and manual exit selection, connecting existing clients to your own management environment.

  1. Sign in to the member portal

    Review the subscription plan, device allowance and available exits, and manage your devices.

  2. Set up the official app

    Follow the member guide to install the official NetBird app, set your service’s management URL and sign in with the same identity.

  3. Choose an exit

    Once the device is authorized, manually select an exit in the app. Return to the member portal to review usage and fair-use status.
