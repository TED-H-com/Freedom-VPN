## Schedule and qualification declared before the run

The observer now calls the pure Go `boundaryplan.Plan` function. The D−1 s checkpoint, copied calibration inputs, both original cadences, added grid anchor, median latency correction, exclusions and maximum-transit formula are unchanged. Every calibration sample is retained; **discounted samples: none**. The plan always emits `added_scheduled_mono_ns` as an array. It sets `qualified:true` only for a nonempty array. An unqualified result includes its reason, unrounded and rounded spacing, actual cadence gaps, and **every candidate with every original send that excluded it**.

The conservative trade-off is deliberate: an isolated capture-read outlier may make a sample unqualified. That happened in **one of these nine sampled runs**; this small, heterogeneous table is not a reliable future failure-rate estimate. Reducing spacing below a real transit could only increase rejection under D-142's captured-order rule; it could never authorize a false pass. No smaller spacing or sample discount is introduced here.

D-142 still judges **every adjacent ingress-ordered pair from the earlier of the last positive and first drop through sweep end**. Earlier positive-positive overlaps are recorded without changing the judged scope. The byte-identical `deadline_trace.go` still requires a positive at or after D−101 ms, no later positive after the first drop, and first drop by D+100 ms. All attempts count. A nonempty planned schedule does not itself establish captured-order qualification. **No retry.** The follow-up 24 member-expiry plan is unchanged.

The reviewer validates plan fields before consuming them, retains every existing check and its wording, writes `boundary-review.json` with `status:fail` and `schedule_qualified:false` for unqualified or malformed plans, and exits 1. It reports the original packet verdict independently. Missing/null/empty/non-list schedules, missing/wrongly typed fields, malformed JSON and a missing plan file are covered.
