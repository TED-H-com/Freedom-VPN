## What Held

- **Concurrent revoke and admit at the slot limit (`TestReview_Held_ConcurrentRevokeAndAdmitSlotInvariant` in `internal/devices/review_d052_integration_test.go`):** Both `RevokeDevice` (`internal/devices/service.go`) and `AuthorizeDevice` (`internal/devices/service.go`) call `Entitlements.Recompute`, which acquires `SELECT ... FROM members WHERE id=$1 FOR UPDATE`. Concurrent revoke of `peer-a-1`, admit of `peer-a-2`, and cross-member revoke attempt against `peer-b-1` serialize cleanly on the member row lock, ending with `authorized_count <= 1` for Member A and `authorized_count == 1` for Member B.
- **Mid-admission suspension and expiry race (`TestReview_Held_MidAdmissionSuspensionAndExpiryCannotSlipThrough` in `internal/devices/review_d052_integration_test.go`):** Observing a peer before suspension/expiry and calling `AuthorizeDevice` after suspension/expiry fails closed with `ErrEntitlement` (enforced both in `AuthorizeDevice` and by PostgreSQL triggers `check_device_authorization` and `zz_billing_device_interval`). Admitting a peer 1ms before suspension and running `SyncDevice` after suspension re-reads `entitlements`, computes `allow = false`, and withdraws NetBird access instead of granting it.
- **Cross-member isolation and 404/409 parity (`TestReview_Held_CrossMemberIsolationAnd404Parity` in `internal/members/httpadapter/review_d052_integration_test.go`):** `GET /api/v1/member/me` and `GET /api/v1/member/devices` never leak another member's UUID, device UUIDs, subjects, peer IDs, or exit group UUIDs. `DELETE /api/v1/member/devices/{device_id}` checks `c.Actor.ID != d.MemberID` before checking `d.Status == "revoked"`, returning identical `404 RESOURCE_NOT_FOUND` responses whether the target UUID belongs to another member's active device, another member's already-revoked device, or does not exist. `GET /api/v1/member/operations/{operation_id}` checks `principal != "member:"+viewer.ID.String()`, returning identical `404 RESOURCE_NOT_FOUND` responses for another member's operation, an admin operation, or a random UUID.
- **OIDC PKCE, state/nonce binding, callback replay, session fixation, CSRF logout, and cookie flags (`TestAT28_OIDCVerificationAndSessions` in `internal/auth/browser_test.go` & `TestID01_ScopedIdentitySessionRotationAndExpiry` in `internal/auth/store_integration_test.go`):** `ConsumeFlow` atomically consumes login state on the first callback, preventing callback token/code replay and binding `state` to the `__Host-fvpn-oidc` cookie. Mismatched `iss`, missing `aud`, wrong `nonce`, wrong PKCE `code_verifier`, expired tokens, future `auth_time`, and admin logins without MFA (`amr`/`acr`) are rejected before any session or identity row is written. Immutability triggers in `db/migrations/00004_guards.sql` and `00005_evidence_and_deadlines.sql` prevent reassigning an existing binding or device to another member.

## Detailed Review Notes (Archived from `.agent/REVIEW.md`)

### `F-01` [High]: Plan downgrade and post-suspension re-admission ignore `device_limit`, leaving excess devices authorized in DB, NetBird, and node `DesiredStateEnvelope`
- **Where:** `internal/entitlement/store.go:168-172` (`Store.Recompute`), `internal/devices/worker.go:65` (`Service.SyncDevice`), `internal/desiredstate/interfaces.go:471-525` (`DBEntitlementReader.ListEntitledMembersForGroups`), `internal/devices/service.go:257-283` (`Service.List`).
- **Scenario:** A member on a 3-slot plan admits and syncs 3 devices and then transitions to a 1-slot plan. `Entitlements.Recompute` updates `entitlements.device_limit = 1` without deauthorizing excess `member_devices` rows and extends `device_bindings.valid_until` for all 3 devices; `SyncDevice` and `DBEntitlementReader.ListEntitledMembersForGroups` keep all 3 devices active in NetBird and in compiled node `DesiredStateEnvelope`s.
- **Proof:** `TestReview_F01_PlanDowngradeAndUnsuspendExceedDeviceSlots` (`internal/members/httpadapter/review_d052_integration_test.go`), `TestReview_F01_CompilerEmitsExcessDeviceBindingsAfterPlanDowngrade` (`apps/worker/review_d052_integration_test.go`).
- **Fix (#125):** Deterministically keeps the oldest `device_limit` authorized devices (`authorized_at ASC, id ASC`), withdraws excess devices and closes their `device_bindings` intervals on downgrade, suspension, and grant revocation, and re-admits within the exact quota.

### `F-02` [High]: Removing an exit group from a member leaves stale `DeviceBindings` and `MemberRatePolicies` authorized on nodes serving the removed group
- **Where:** `internal/desiredstate/compiler.go:882-904` (`Compiler.RecompileBatch`), `internal/entitlement/store.go:188-199` (`Store.Recompute`).
- **Scenario:** When an exit group is removed from a member, `Entitlements.Recompute` enqueues `member.entitlement_changed` containing only the new `exit_group_ids`, so `Compiler.RecompileBatch` recompiles only nodes in the remaining groups and leaves nodes in the removed group authorizing the member.
- **Proof:** `TestReview_F02_ExitGroupRemovalLeavesStaleDeviceBindingOnRemovedGroupNode` (`apps/worker/review_d052_integration_test.go`).
- **Fix (#109, #125):** Included the union of old and new `exit_group_ids` on `member.entitlement_changed` and queried nodes whose current `desired_state_envelopes` still carry the member's bindings or rate policies so removed-group nodes are recompiled immediately.

### `F-03` [High]: Advancing `netbird_user_bindings.revision` (`proof_updated`) permanently breaks `NetBirdAdapter.SetAuthorization` with `netbird: conflict`
- **Where:** `internal/netbird/operations.go:140-150` (`Client.ensureMember`), `internal/devices/netbird_adapter.go:122,173`, `internal/devices/identity.go:136-145`.
- **Scenario:** `Client.ensureMember` hashed `[IdentityBindingID, MappingRevision, UserID]` into `external_bindings.desired_hash`, so advancing `netbird_user_bindings.revision` from `1` to `2` caused every subsequent `SyncDevice` to fail with `netbird: conflict`.
- **Proof:** `TestReview_F03_D052ProofRevisionAdvanceBricksDeviceSync` (`internal/members/httpadapter/review_d052_integration_test.go`).
- **Fix (#125):** Kept the member identity proof stable across `proof_updated` revisions while still rejecting genuinely changed identity tuples, and upgraded pre-existing proof hashes through database evidence.

### `F-04` [High]: `identity_bindings` uniqueness is scoped to `(issuer, client_scope, subject)` instead of `(issuer, subject)`
- **Where:** `db/migrations/00001_members.sql:23`, `internal/auth/store.go:62-75`, `internal/devices/identity.go:74-96,150-153`.
- **Scenario:** Logging in with the same `(issuer, subject)` after an OIDC `client_id` rotation (`client_scope` change) created a duplicate `members` row and a second `identity_bindings` row, breaking `verifiedIdentity` (`NOT EXISTS ... other.issuer=i.issuer AND other.subject=i.subject`) for the first member.
- **Proof:** `TestReview_F04_CrossClientScopeCreatesDuplicateMemberAndBricksD052` (`internal/devices/review_d052_integration_test.go`).
- **Fix (#114, #122, #125, #134, #137):** Keyed login on canonical `(issuer, subject)` under an advisory lock (`#114`), enforced `UNIQUE(issuer, subject)` in migration `00183` (`#125`), made `client_scope` mutable diagnostic metadata in migration `00186` (`#134`), and updated `client_scope` and `verified_at` in place with one `identity_binding.proof_updated` audit per rotation (`#137`).

### `F-05`–`F-11` [High / Medium]: OIDC `azp` check, `device_bindings` clamping on suspend/revoke, `D-029` admission and allowlisted audits, SQL `mapping_proof` check, `issuer.Canonical`, `SyncMember` error accumulation, and cross-principal logout / dual-role login
- **Proofs:** `TestReview_F05_OIDCCallbackAcceptsMismatchedAzpAndMultiAudienceTokens` (`internal/auth/review_d052_test.go`), `TestReview_F06_SuspensionAndGrantRevokeKeepActiveDeviceBindingsBlockingIPReuse`, `TestReview_F08_RuntimeAdapterNilIdentityAllowsUnverifiedMappingProof`, `TestReview_F09_IssuerNormalizationMismatchFailsD052Discovery`, `TestReview_F10_SyncMemberEarlyAbortLeavesRemainingDevicesAuthorizedOnSuspend` (`internal/devices/review_d052_integration_test.go`), `TestReview_F07_D029FailedAdmissionsUnauditedAndUnallowlistedAuditPayloads` (`internal/members/httpadapter/review_d052_integration_test.go`), `TestReview_F11_CrossPrincipalSessionOverwriteUnauditedAndAdminClaimBlocksMemberBinding` (`internal/auth/review_d052_integration_test.go`).
- **Fix (#106, #125, #128, #137, #139):** Validated `azp` in `/api/v1/auth/callback` (`#106`); persisted login flow `role` in `oidc_login_flows` (`#106`, migration `00185` in `#128`) and audited cross-principal `session.logout` (`#106`); clamped `device_bindings.valid_until` on suspension/grant revocation, audited rejected device admissions on the `D-029` allowlist with formal `operation_id`, verified `mapping_proof` in SQL, canonicalized issuers via `internal/members/issuer`, and attempted all devices in `SyncMember` (`#125`, `#137`, `#139`).
